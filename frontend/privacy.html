Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to KickCutter (accessible at https://kickcutter.pages.dev). We are dedicated to providing a fast, secure, and privacy-respecting video clipping tool for Kick.com streams, VODs, and clips.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our service. In short: we do not collect personal identities, we do not permanently store videos, and we never sell user data.

2. Information We Process

  • Video URLs & Timestamps: When you submit a Kick URL and time range, our servers process that specific request to slice the requested segment.
  • Server Execution Logs: We maintain standard temporary technical server logs (such as HTTP request status, timestamp, and anonymous IP address) for rate-limiting, abuse prevention, and performance debugging.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need an account, password, or credit card to use KickCutter.

3. How Clips Are Handled (Zero Permanent Storage)

KickCutter does not store any stream or VOD video permanently:

  • When a clip is generated, it is stored in a temporary server cache solely so you can download it.
  • Temporary clip files are automatically purged and deleted after download or within a maximum of 2 hours.
  • We do not archive, catalog, or share your trimmed clips with anyone else.

4. Cookies and Tracking

KickCutter does not use tracking cookies, behavioral tracking scripts, or cross-site fingerprinting. Standard browser session storage may be used strictly to retain your user UI preferences (such as quality settings).

5. Third-Party Services

Our website interfaces with Cloudflare Pages (for frontend content delivery) and Hugging Face infrastructure (for backend processing). These providers operate under their respective privacy and security standards.

6. Disclaimer & Affiliation

KickCutter is an independent, open-source utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kick.com or its parent company.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data inquiries, please reach out via our Contact Page.
