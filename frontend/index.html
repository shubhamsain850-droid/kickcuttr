Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about clipping on Kick.

How do I get the link of a Kick stream or VOD? +
Open the Kick broadcast or VOD in your browser and copy the URL from the address bar (e.g. https://kick.com/video/UUID or https://kick.com/channelname).
Do the downloaded clips have watermarks? +
No! Every clip exported via KickCutter is 100% clean and free of watermarks or logos, making it ideal for content creators editing for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.
What is the maximum clip duration allowed? +
You can slice segments up to 2 hours long in a single clip. For social media highlights, segments between 30 seconds and 3 minutes process fastest.
Can I cut a clip while the streamer is still live? +
Yes! If the streamer has VOD recordings enabled, you can enter the channel URL and clip recent broadcast segments while the stream is in progress.
Is KickCutter affiliated with Kick.com? +
No, KickCutter is an independent open-source utility created for video editors and fans. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kick.com.
