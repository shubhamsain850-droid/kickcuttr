Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about clipping on Kick.

How do I get the link of a Kick stream or VOD? +
Open the Kick broadcast, VOD, or clip in your browser and copy the URL from the address bar (e.g. https://kick.com/xqc or https://kick.com/video/UUID).
Do the downloaded clips have watermarks? +
No! Every clip exported via KickCutter is 100% clean and free of watermarks or logos, making it ideal for content creators editing for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.
How does the range slider work? +
Drag the left handle to set the starting moment and the right handle to set the ending moment. You can also type timestamps directly or click quick preset buttons like +30s or +1min.
Will the download start automatically when ready? +
Yes! As soon as the progress bar reaches 100%, your browser will automatically download the MP4 clip, and a download button is also provided for re-downloading anytime.
Is KickCutter affiliated with Kick.com? +
No, KickCutter is an independent open-source creator tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kick.com.
