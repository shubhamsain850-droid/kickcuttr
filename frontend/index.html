Frequently Asked Questions

Categorized answers to everything you need to know about downloading and cutting on Kick.

How do I do a Kick video clip cut online? +
Simply paste the Kick video or stream URL into KickCutter, click 'Fetch Info', adjust the range slider to select your exact start and end timestamps, and click 'Cut Clip Now'. Your high-quality MP4 clip will download automatically.
Can I do a long stream clip cut on 8+ hour Kick broadcasts? +
Yes! KickCutter is built specifically for long stream clip cutting. Instead of downloading full 20GB+ stream recordings, our cloud server slices only the exact segment you select, saving bandwidth and device storage.
Can I cut a specific part of a Kick VOD? +
Yes. Enter the start and end timestamps or drag the interactive range slider to extract the exact portion of an accessible Kick VOD or stream.
Can I download only one minute of a Kick stream? +
Yes. Set the start timestamp and select a 60-second duration using our quick length presets (+1 min) or slider.
Can I cut a Kick VOD without downloading the entire video? +
Yes! KickCutter performs server-side stream slicing. It fetches only the required video segments, saving your internet bandwidth and local storage.
What is the maximum clip duration allowed? +
You can slice segments up to 2 hours long in a single clip. For social media highlights and Shorts, segments between 30 seconds and 3 minutes process fastest.
Can I download a Kick clip in its original quality? +
Yes. Select "Best Available Source" or "1080p60 Full HD" in the quality dropdown to preserve the streamer's exact original bitrate and framerate.
Can I cut a Kick live stream while the streamer is still live? +
Yes! If the streamer has VOD recordings enabled, you can enter the channel URL and clip recent broadcast segments while the stream is in progress.
What is KickCutter? +
KickCutter is an online tool for downloading public Kick VODs and clips, cutting specific video segments, and exporting watermark-free MP4 videos in up to 1080p resolution.
Is KickCutter free to use? +
KickCutter is 100% free with no registration required. Usage may be subject to server capacity limits for ultra-long recordings.
How do I download a Kick VOD? +
Copy the URL of a public Kick VOD, paste it into KickCutter, click 'Fetch Info', inspect the video preview, select your resolution, and click 'Cut Clip Now'.
Can I download Kick videos in MP4 format? +
Yes. All video clips and VOD segments are generated in standard H.264 MP4 format, compatible with iPhone, Android, Premiere Pro, and all editing software.
Can I download Kick videos in 1080p? +
Yes! KickCutter autofetches all resolutions provided by the streamer, allowing you to export in 1080p60 Full HD, 720p60, 480p, and original source quality.
Can I download deleted Kick VODs? +
No. KickCutter cannot access videos or recordings that have been deleted or removed by Kick or the streamer.
Does KickCutter add a watermark? +
No! All downloads and cuts are completely clean and 100% watermark-free, ready for your creative edits.
Does KickCutter work on mobile devices? +
Yes. KickCutter is fully responsive and works smoothly through modern mobile browsers on iPhone (iOS Safari) and Android (Chrome).
Do I need to install FFmpeg or any software? +
No. All video processing, FFmpeg slicing, and MP4 packaging occur remotely on our high-speed cloud servers.
Why is video processing taking a few seconds? +
Processing time depends on clip duration, selected resolution, stream bitrate, and server load. Most clips under 3 minutes process in under 15 seconds.
Can I download chat from a past Kick stream? +
Kick.com operates chat purely over real-time WebSockets and does not provide public historical chat replay API endpoints for ended VODs. On-screen streamer chat burned into the video is preserved, but independent text chat export for past VODs is not supported by Kick.
Why does my clip start a few seconds earlier or later? +
Video streams use keyframes (I-frames) every few seconds. KickCutter uses keyframe-aware cuts to ensure playback is smooth and artifact-free without audio desync.
Are downloaded files stored permanently? +
No. Generated MP4 clips are temporary and are automatically deleted from server memory after download to protect user privacy.
Can I redistribute downloaded Kick streams? +
You should respect copyright laws and obtain permission from the original streamer or rights holder before distributing their content commercially.
Is KickCutter officially affiliated with Kick.com? +
No. KickCutter is an independent open-source utility created for video editors and fans. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Kick.com.
